Mini-GPT

Python PyTorch Causal Attention CUDA
What It Does

A from-scratch deep learning implementation of a Generative Pre-trained Transformer (GPT) language model, built without third-party LLM frameworks to investigate and master transformer mechanics.

How I Made It & Architecture

Constructed decoder-only transformer blocks in PyTorch: multi-head causal self-attention, learnable positional and token embeddings, layer normalization, residual skip-connections, and GELU feed-forward networks. Built custom tokenization, AdamW training with cosine learning rate schedules, and autoregressive generation with temperature and top-k sampling.

Key Engineering Highlights
  • 100% written in PyTorch from mathematical fundamentals
  • Autoregressive sampling with temperature & top-k controls
